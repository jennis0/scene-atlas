Variant 1 — The Tome

No sidebar at all. Categories are "chapters" — Cinzel headings you can open and close like an actual reference tome. The warm dark palette (very subtly amber-tinted) separates it from the cold navy of the main UI, giving the filter panel its own micro-atmosphere. Active filters show as amber dots on the chapter header.

Variant 2 — The Cartographer's Legend

The left nav becomes a compact icon rail — each category reduced to a coloured tile + label, like symbols in a map legend. More visual, less listy. The right content area gets more horizontal space as a result, so pills can breathe. The category name appears as a large Cinzel heading in the content header, making the current context immediately clear.
