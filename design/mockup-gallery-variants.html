Gallery Grid Variants

Three approaches to the gallery card pattern — addressing tag density, subgroup sections, and interaction

B1 — Hover Reveal
Cards show type/grid controls at all times in a thin strip. Tags are hidden by default and slide open on hover or selection — keeping cards compact when browsing, detailed when you focus. Subgroups use sticky horizontal dividers. Handles many tags gracefully because they only appear when you're actually looking at a card.

B2 — Badge Overlay
Type and grid status shown as small pill badges overlaid on the thumbnail's top-left corner. Tags in a single-line strip below the card with an overflow count (+N) for cards with many tags. Subgroups get bold Cinzel headings with an amber accent bar. Maximizes thumbnail visibility while keeping metadata scannable.

B3 — Expanding Cards
Cards are thumbnail-only by default — extremely compact, showing maximum images at once. Clicking a card selects it AND expands it to reveal full controls and tags. The selected card spans two columns to make room. Subgroups use a vertical rail with rotated labels as persistent section markers. Best density when browsing; full control when editing.
